# Marketing Motion System

Central tokens define instant, fast, standard, slow, and atmospheric durations plus standard, enter, exit, and fluid easing curves.

Organic forms use layered radial material, asymmetric radii, internal highlights, edge definition, and a long irregular fluid-drop cycle. Route changes update the curated preset without delaying navigation; the persistent shell fades and settles the field around the real Next.js transition.

Pointer input is requestAnimationFrame-throttled and limited to 12 by 9 pixels on fine-pointer, full-tier devices. Mobile and constrained devices render fewer forms. Reduced-motion users receive a static composition.
