# Alphaclone Brand Messaging Prompt Checklist

> Based on Cialdini's Influence + Sutherland's Alchemy + BS Continuum Framework

Use this checklist before publishing ANY copy: landing pages, emails, ads, social posts, UI microcopy, onboarding flows, notifications.

---

## Global Rules

- [ ] Does this copy **educate** the reader (benefits them) rather than **manipulate** them (benefits us at their expense)?
- [ ] Is every claim **specific and verifiable**? (numbers, names, outcomes -- not vague superlatives)
- [ ] Would I be comfortable if a cynical, skeptical journalist read this and fact-checked it?
- [ ] Does this copy **respect the reader's intelligence**?
- [ ] Is the **emotional appeal grounded in a real product truth**? (emotional alchemy, not emotional deception)

---

## Pillar 1: Reciprocity

- [ ] Are we **giving genuine value before asking** for anything (email, trial, purchase)?
- [ ] Is the free thing **useful without signing up** -- or is it a disguised lead capture?
- [ ] Are free resources (templates, audits, content) **ours to give** -- not repurposed/scraped?
- [ ] Is the free offer **clearly described** with no hidden strings in the small print?
- [ ] Does the **effort visible to the user** (packaging, design, detail) match the actual quality of what's inside?

**Red flags to remove:**

- "Get your free [X]" where X requires credit card
- Fake "exclusive access" for newsletter signups
- Free trials that auto-charge without clear notice

---

## Pillar 2: Social Proof

- [ ] Are all testimonials and case studies **real, named, and verifiable**?
- [ ] Do the metrics quoted (e.g. "saved 12 hours/week") come from **actual customer data**?
- [ ] Is the social proof **relevant to the prospect** reading it (same industry, size, problem)?
- [ ] Are user counts / customer numbers **accurate and up to date**?
- [ ] Have we avoided **cherry-picked outlier results** presented as typical outcomes?
- [ ] If using a celebrity or influencer, do they **genuinely use the product**?
- [ ] Are "as seen in" logos from **meaningful coverage**, not a 1-line listicle mention?

**Red flags to remove:**

- Generic stock-photo testimonials
- Round numbers ("1000+ customers") that haven't been updated in 12 months
- "Average results may vary" buried in tiny print after a huge claim

---

## Pillar 3: Authority

- [ ] Is the authority figure **actually an expert in this domain** (not borrowed credibility)?
- [ ] What does the authority figure **gain** from this endorsement -- is that disclosed?
- [ ] Do certifications, badges, or awards **link to the actual source/certificate**?
- [ ] Is the "Trust" or "Security" page up to date with **real documentation**?
- [ ] Is thought-leadership content based on **original insight or data** -- not generic advice?
- [ ] Does visual design convey quality **consistent with actual product quality** (no fake vents)?

**Red flags to remove:**

- Vague "industry-leading" or "enterprise-grade" with no evidence
- Broken links to certifications
- Logos of tools you "integrate with" that don't actually work yet

---

## Pillar 4: Scarcity

- [ ] Is the scarcity **real** (limited team capacity, genuine early-bird pricing, real deadline)?
- [ ] If using a countdown timer, does it **actually expire** and not reset?
- [ ] Is the reason for scarcity **explained honestly** to the user?
- [ ] Are "spots remaining" counters **accurate and updated in real time**?
- [ ] Is urgency based on **benefit to the customer** (lock in lower price) not just pressure?
- [ ] Would we be comfortable if the customer came back the next day and the "limited offer" was gone?

**Red flags to remove:**

- "Only 3 left!" on a SaaS product with unlimited seats
- Evergreen countdown timers that reset on page reload
- "Price goes up Friday" emails sent every week

---

## Pillar 5: Unity / Tribe

- [ ] Is the shared identity built around **a real problem we solve**, not just vibes?
- [ ] Does our community (Discord, Slack) provide **genuine value** beyond being a sales funnel?
- [ ] Are we entering any social/political topic because we **genuinely believe it** or just for engagement?
- [ ] Does our tribal language ("Alphaclone builders") feel **earned by the user**, not forced?
- [ ] Are we careful not to make customers feel they **must defend us** on social media?
- [ ] Are brand values **reflected in our business practices** (not just marketing copy)?

**Red flags to remove:**

- "Join the family!" with no actual community behind it
- Performative social stances that contradict internal practices
- Loyalty programs that punish leaving rather than reward staying

---

## Visual / Design Copy Audit

- [ ] Does packaging/UI design **honestly represent** what the product does?
- [ ] Are visual signals (icons, badges, progress bars) **grounded in real functionality**?
- [ ] Does the design quality **match the product quality** -- neither over- nor under-promising?
- [ ] Are pricing page "most popular" or "best value" labels **based on actual data**?
- [ ] Are dark patterns absent: pre-ticked boxes, hidden fees, confusing cancel flows?

---

## Channel-Specific Checks

### Landing Pages

- [ ] Hero claim supported within the first scroll
- [ ] Social proof above the fold (real, specific)
- [ ] CTA describes exactly what happens next ("Start free 14-day trial -- no credit card")
- [ ] Pricing is transparent with no hidden fees revealed at checkout

### Cold Outreach / Email Sequences

- [ ] Value offered in email 1 before any ask
- [ ] Personalisation is genuine, not mail-merge that breaks
- [ ] Unsubscribe is 1-click and actually works
- [ ] Follow-up sequences have a clear stop condition (not infinite)

### Social Media

- [ ] Claims can be substantiated if someone asks
- [ ] Engagement bait ("comment YES if you agree") used sparingly
- [ ] Results shared include context (industry, team size, timeline)

### In-App / Onboarding

- [ ] Progress indicators are accurate -- don't show 80% complete when there are 10 steps left
- [ ] Upgrade prompts explain **why this feature benefits the user** specifically
- [ ] Notifications are actionable and not just re-engagement spam

---

## Final BS Continuum Score

Before publishing, rate this piece of copy:

| Score | Meaning                                   | Action           |
| ----- | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| 1-2   | Pure information, fully honest            | Publish          |
| 3-4   | Emotional framing of real benefits        | Publish          |
| 5-6   | Selective truth, pressure tactics present | Revise           |
| 7-8   | Misleading claims, fake social proof      | Do not publish   |
| 9-10  | Fabricated data, outright deception       | Legal/brand risk |

**Target: all Alphaclone copy scores 1-4.**
