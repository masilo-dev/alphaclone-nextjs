# Marketing Atmosphere Audit

The public marketing system is mounted once in `MarketingShell` and selects a curated preset from the active route. The authenticated dashboard is not included.

| Page family | Previous visual problem | Blue usage | Preset | Motion | Mobile | Reduced motion | Performance | Route | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Home | Four synchronized blurred ellipses | Dominant | Hero | Balanced | Two forms, no transition wash | Static material | Tiered | Live | Complete |
| Product and integrations | Same background on every page | High | Product | Subtle | Simplified | Static material | Tiered | Live | Complete |
| Pricing, contact and auth | No conversion-specific focus | High | Conversion | Subtle | Simplified | Static material | Tiered | Live | Complete |
| Blog, guide, docs and about | Product atmosphere competed with reading | High | Editorial | Subtle | Simplified | Static material | Tiered | Live | Complete |
| Legal, privacy and security | Decorative color distracted from policy text | High | Quiet | Minimal | One to two forms | Static material | Tiered | Live | Complete |
| Other public pages | No route identity | High | Dark | Subtle | Simplified | Static material | Tiered | Live | Complete |

The new foundation is charcoal and warm neutral. Coral and teal are used as small atmospheric moments; blue is reserved for existing informational/product states.
