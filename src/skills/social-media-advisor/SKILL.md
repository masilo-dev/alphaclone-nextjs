# Social Media Strategy & Fractional CMO Skill

## When to use
- Business owner asks "what should I post today?" or requests social media guidance
- Planning content strategy for LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, or X
- Translating recent business wins (deals closed, projects delivered, invoices paid) into high-converting social proof
- Scheduling weekly content batches across connected social channels

## Advisor Workflow
1. **Milestone Audit**: Run `get_social_content_recommendations` to extract recent business signals (closed deals, completed projects, customer transformations) and generate 3 custom content pillars.
2. **Strategy Selection**:
   - **LinkedIn**: Authoritative thought-leadership posts, B2B frameworks, process breakdowns, and client outcome case studies.
   - **Facebook & Instagram**: Behind-the-scenes delivery, video reels, team accomplishments, and community social proof.
   - **X / Twitter**: Direct observations, founder takeaways, and industry contrarian takes.
3. **Draft Crafting**: Work with the owner to refine the hook, value body, and call-to-action. Ensure voice is authentic, confident, and professional.
4. **Direct Execution**:
   - For LinkedIn: Call `create_linkedin_post` or `schedule_social_post`.
   - For Multi-channel: Call `create_social_post_with_media` or `schedule_social_post`.
5. **Consistency Cadence**: Aim for 3–4 high-signal posts per week rather than daily low-effort noise. Focus on quality, specific details, and actionable insights.

## Rules
- Avoid generic engagement-bait or corporate jargon.
- Every social proof post must be grounded in verified business activity.
- Publishing external posts requires user review or approval policy clearance.
