# Route map

## CRM

- `/dashboard/crm`: CRM overview and module entry.
- `/dashboard/crm/workspace`: operational CRM workspace (lead, customer, and contact management).
- `/dashboard/crm/unified-contacts`: unified records.
- `/dashboard/crm/accounts`: accounts.
- `/dashboard/crm/follow-ups`: follow-up queue.
- `/dashboard/crm/console`: sales console.
- `/dashboard/crm/reports`: CRM reporting.

## Adjacent modules requiring the same visual standard

- `/dashboard/leads`: lead discovery and pipeline entry.
- `/dashboard/deals`: deal management.
- `/dashboard/tasks`: task execution.
- `/dashboard/projects`: project delivery.
- `/dashboard/mail`: messaging.
- `/dashboard/social`: social publishing.
- `/dashboard/documents`: documents.
- `/dashboard/business/*`: finance and business operations.

The CRM redesign is the reference implementation. Shared tokens and layout fixes should subsequently make adjacent modules visually consistent without changing their behavior.
