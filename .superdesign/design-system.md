# Alphaclone OS design system

Preserve the established dark operating-system identity and all business functionality.

Use a near-black atmospheric canvas with subtle navy/blue ambience visible behind content. Build hierarchy with translucent graphite surfaces, quiet white borders, soft black depth, and restrained blur. Never cover the full module viewport with an opaque blue or slate layer.

Blue is the CRM identity and primary interaction color. Use it for focus, selection, active navigation, and primary actions only. Use neutral surfaces for most containers. Keep semantic emerald, amber, rose, violet, and indigo roles distinct.

Use a consistent responsive spacing system: 24px desktop outer gutter, 16px tablet, 12px mobile; 20px between major sections; 8–12px between related controls. Outer surfaces use 16–20px radii, controls 10–12px, and pills only for concise filters/status.

Preserve:

- CRM command actions and sync states
- KPIs, pipeline integrity, and client pulse
- Leads/customers/contacts tabs and counts
- Search, filters, list/board views, import, selection, bulk actions
- Table/mobile cards, kanban, drawers, modals, empty/loading/error states
- Keyboard navigation, focus visibility, contrast, and 44px touch targets

Target a premium, calm, highly legible enterprise workspace—not a generic card dashboard. The atmospheric canvas should add character while the operational content remains dense and easy to scan.
